Frequently asked questions

Do you also manage emails and ads?

Yes. We offer Email Management (inbox cleanup, labels, filters and reply drafts) and Ads Manager (Facebook, Instagram and Google ads setup, monitoring and reporting). Book a free call and we will quote it based on your volume and ad budget. Ad spend itself is paid by you directly to the ad platform.

How much does it cost?

₱2,000 one time, as our launch price. You get a custom 4-6 page website, SEO setup, Google Business Profile setup and 2 rounds of revisions. We start the build after a 50% deposit, and you pay the balance only after you approve the design. Ongoing site care is optional: the first month is free, then ₱1,400 a month.

Who owns the code?

You do. Once the balance is paid, the website code and your content are yours. If you ever want to move to another host or developer, we hand everything over.

What are the hosting costs?

Your ₱2,000 covers the design and the build. The domain name and hosting are separate running costs paid to the providers. We tell you the exact amount before you pay the deposit, so there are no surprises later.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds of revisions are included. A round is one list of changes that you send us together. If you need more than two, we agree the price with you before we start.

How long does it take?

Most sites are ready for your review within 7-10 days of us receiving your content and photos. The exact timing depends on the number of pages and how quickly feedback comes back.

What does maintenance include?

Ongoing updates and fixes, security patches, backups and uptime monitoring, so your site stays current, secure and online. The first month is free, then it is an optional ₱1,400 a month. New pages and major new features are quoted separately.

What if I don't like the design?

You approve the design before you pay the balance. We start with a 50% deposit, you review the design, and you pay the rest only when you are happy with it.
