Founder & Web Developer at 404 Studio · Quezon City, Philippines

I specialize in designing and developing custom websites that bring together visual storytelling, thoughtful user experience, and functional web solutions. My work spans responsive website design, front-end development, interactive interfaces, content-driven platforms, portfolio websites, and e-commerce concepts. I focus on transforming ideas into polished digital experiences through careful attention to layout, typography, visual hierarchy, accessibility, and performance across desktop and mobile devices.

Beyond design and development, I work on the practical side of maintaining a website—from structuring content and refining user interactions to troubleshooting issues, improving responsiveness, and implementing updates. I believe a successful website should do more than look good; it should communicate clearly, function reliably, and give visitors a seamless experience.

Through 404 Studio, I aim to make thoughtful, professionally designed websites more accessible to small businesses, independent creators, entrepreneurs, and emerging brands. My approach combines creativity with problem-solving, building digital spaces that reflect each project's identity while remaining adaptable, intuitive, and purposeful.
