FAQ

Common questions

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first 30-minute case review is free and confidential, with no obligation to hire us.

After the consultation we give you a written fee estimate. We offer fixed fees for many services and payment plans where possible.

Is what I tell you confidential?

Yes. Everything you share with us is protected by attorney-client privilege.

We reply to every inquiry within one business day. Urgent matters, such as an arrest, are handled right away.
