Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about physical therapy sessions and expectations.

Do I need a doctor's referral to book?

Direct access allows you to seek physical therapy without a referral. However, some insurance policies require one for reimbursement—check with your provider for specific details.

How long does a session last?

Standard therapy sessions last 45 to 60 minutes. Your initial evaluation may take slightly longer so we can conduct a comprehensive assessment.

Will physical therapy treatment be painful?

Some interventions might cause mild soreness as muscles work in new ways, but your therapist continually monitors and adjusts intensity to match your comfort level.

How many sessions will I need?

The total number of sessions depends on your specific condition, health goals, and progress. Your therapist will provide an estimated timeline following your initial assessment.
