Good questions


How does REY.AI work?

REY.AI is our pet care assistant. It answers common questions, points you to the right tool and helps you book. It does not diagnose, so your vet decides on health concerns.

When is it an emergency?

Trouble breathing, collapse, seizures, heavy bleeding, suspected poisoning or severe pain. Call us immediately.

Do you see exotic pets?

Yes, including rabbits, birds and small pets. Tell us the type when booking.
